# Careers in Cyber

{% embed url="https://tryhackme.com/room/careersincyber" %}

An exploratory overview of career pathways in information security: Penetration Testing (Offensive/Red Team), Incident Response (Blue Team), and Security Operations Center (SOC) Analysis.

---

## 1. Industry Landscape

The cybersecurity sector faces a massive global skills shortage:
- A computer science degree is **not** strictly mandatory to build a successful career in cyber.
- Millions of positions remain open globally, accessible to individuals from varied analytical, technical, and non-traditional backgrounds.

---

## 2. Core Career Domains

| Role | Focus | Common Tools |
|---|---|---|
| **Penetration Tester / Red Teamer** | Ethical exploitation of vulnerabilities to assess resilience | Nmap, Burp Suite, Metasploit |
| **Security Analyst (SOC)** | Monitoring real-time alerts, triaging SIEM logs, detecting intrusions | Splunk, ELK, Snort, Wireshark |
| **Incident Responder** | Containing breaches, forensic investigation, root-cause recovery | Volatility, Autopsy, KAPE |
