## 1. Blue Team Organization & Hierarchy

- **CISO (Chief Information Security Officer):** Executive leadership responsible for enterprise-wide risk management, strategic policies, and high-level cybersecurity decisions.
- **Blue Team:** Collective term for operational defense personnel (SOC analysts, security engineers, threat hunters).
- **CIRT (Cyber Incident Response Team):** Specialized SWAT unit activated to manage high-severity active breaches.

---

## 2. Industry Service Models

- **MSSP (Managed Security Service Provider):** External cybersecurity firm providing outsourced 24/7 SOC monitoring, detection, and incident response services to client organizations.
- **Career Ladder:**
  - **Tier 1 (SOC L1 Analyst):** Alert triage and initial classification.
  - **Tier 2 (SOC L2 Analyst):** In-depth investigation, threat hunting, and incident response handling.
  - **Tier 3 (SOC L3 / Threat Hunter):** Advanced reverse engineering, malware analysis, and proactive hunting.
