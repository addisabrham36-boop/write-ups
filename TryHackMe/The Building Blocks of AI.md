An introduction to Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning paradigms, Deep Learning architectures, Large Language Model transformers, and the MITRE ATLAS adversarial framework.

---

## 1. Machine Learning Paradigms

- **Semi-supervised Learning:** Combines a small volume of labeled data with a large body of unlabeled data during training.
- **Deep Learning:** Multi-layer artificial neural networks capable of autonomous feature extraction from raw, unstructured data without manual feature engineering.
- **Synapses:** Biological brain connections simulated by weighted mathematical parameters between artificial neurons.
- **Input Layer:** The initial neural network layer accepting raw features or pixel data.

---

## 2. Large Language Models (LLMs) & Transformers

- **Transformer Architecture:** Deep neural network architecture introduced by Google in the 2017 paper *"Attention Is All You Need"*, relying on self-attention mechanisms.
- **Pre-training:** The resource-intensive phase where an LLM consumes massive text corpora to learn statistical language patterns before task-specific fine-tuning.

---

## 3. Adversarial AI & MITRE ATLAS

- **MITRE ATLAS:** Adversarial Threat Landscape for Artificial-Intelligence Systems — an open knowledge base of adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures targeting ML systems.
- **Model Theft / Extraction:** Replicating or stealing proprietary model weights or functional capabilities by systematically querying its public API.
- **Deepfakes:** Synthetic media generated using neural generative models to replicate an individual's likeness or voice.
- **Defensive Velocity:** AI-driven automated analytics enable SOC teams to identify and contain breaches up to **108 days faster** according to IBM research.
